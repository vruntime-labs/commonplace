# commonplace.

**Knowledge worth keeping.**

An open knowledge platform for people who build with technology.

Commonplace brings together useful developer resources, practical learning, and an open community for sharing knowledge.

Inspired by the historical commonplace book, it gives scattered knowledge a shared, organised place.

## What is Commonplace?

Building software today means navigating an enormous amount of information: documentation, tools, prompts, AI models, workflows, tutorials, security guidance, and community knowledge.

That information is often scattered, difficult to evaluate, or disconnected from the practical knowledge needed to use it well.

Commonplace aims to bring these pieces together in one open, carefully organised knowledge platform.

## Three pillars

### Discover

Find useful resources for building software and working with AI.

Explore prompts, skills, MCP servers, AI models, developer tools, IDE extensions, workflows, APIs, and configurations.

### Learn

Understand the ideas and practices behind better software.

Learn about programming fundamentals, AI engineering, architecture, security, software quality, accessibility, SEO, deployment, and more.

### Contribute

Help make useful knowledge available to everyone.

Share resources, improve guides, correct information, contribute through GitHub, or submit knowledge through a guided website-based contribution path.

## Our principles

- **Open knowledge:** Useful knowledge should be accessible and shareable.
- **Curated, not merely aggregated:** Sources, licences, relevance, and maintenance matter.
- **Tool agnostic:** No single AI model, IDE, framework, or vendor defines the platform.
- **Beginners welcome, experts equipped:** Make complex subjects approachable without sacrificing technical depth.
- **Honest by design:** Be transparent about sources, limitations, verification, and uncertainty.
- **Community driven:** Build knowledge together and recognize the people who maintain it.
